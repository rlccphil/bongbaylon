About me

I’m a pastor, teacher, life coach, and spiritual director. My goal is to help each one I encounter to become a faithful and fruitful follower of Jesus Christ so that they can fulfill the good works that God wants to do in and through them in the world.

Through guided conversations grounded in Scripture and prayer, I can walk alongside you to help you seek clarity, direction, growth, and maturity in Christ.

My mission in life

  • To help each one become a faithful and fruitful follower of Jesus Christ.
  • To help each one fulfill their fourfold calling in Christ.

If you need help, I invite you to set an appointment with me. Together, let us prayerfully listen to what God may be saying to you, and discern together your next steps.
